SHORT DESCRIPTION
Start 100th in a field of 100 cars on an endless night road and drive to the front. Near misses, scrapes and drifts score style while rival racers and cops chase you.

FEATURES
- Midnight Run: a 100-car field on an endless night road, with rivals, cops, new road types and rain arriving as the run goes on. Pick upgrades at each level-up.
- Solo: eight districts, three of them mountain passes, with traffic density and race options (racers, cops, weather, repair pickups, timed mode, crashes off or Hardcore).
- Online: matchmaking, plus private Crew rooms for 2 to 4 players.
- Four cars with FWD, RWD and AWD handling; automatic or manual shifting with overrev risk.
- Loadout of three abilities (Nitrous, EMP, Siren), 14 aftermarket parts and 8 tire compounds.
