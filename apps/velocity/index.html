Three ways to drive

Midnight Run

The 100-car climb

The core mode. Play it once and the rest of the city opens up.

Solo

The city on your terms

Pick a district and set the traffic. Turn racers, cops, weather, repair pickups and a timed mode on or off. Crashes are off by default; unlock Hardcore and one bad read ends the run.

Crew & Online

Race other people

Queue for online matchmaking, or open a private Crew room for 2 to 4 players. The leader picks the race: a gap race, a timed run against each other, or a timed run together.

A featherweight you have to earn.

Shift automatic, or go manual and risk overrevving the engine. Move power between acceleration, grip and top speed in the middle of a run. Take Nitrous, EMP or Siren as your ability, then tune with 14 aftermarket parts and 8 tire compounds. Paint, rims, underglow, window tint, decals and swappable bumpers, hoods and lights make it yours.

Eight districts, from the City Ring to the mountain passes

The City Ring highway, Uptown, Downtown, Velo City and the Back Streets, then up into the passes of Switchback, Climb and Descent. Tuck in behind a car to catch its slipstream. In a storm, lightning can hit you.

Night city, every route

Where Early Access is going

Velocity is playable today with Midnight Run, Solo, and early Crew and Online multiplayer. Early Access is planned to run roughly 6 to 12 months, and the price stays $5.99 when it ends.

  • Pink slips: keep the car you drove in a Midnight Run, then put it on the line against other players. Win and you take their car. Lose and yours is gone.
  • Local split-screen: two players on one screen.
  • More of the core: more places to drive, cars, abilities and game modes.

These are plans, not promises. Car balance, difficulty and new districts will be shaped by player feedback and bug reports.
