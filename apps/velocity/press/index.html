Description

Velocity - Midnight Run is an arcade night-driving racer. You start 100th in a field of 100 cars on an endless night road and work your way to the front through live traffic. Rival racers join the chase, then the cops, and near misses, scrapes and drifts all pay out as style. Each time you level up you pick abilities and performance parts, so the car you finish with is the one your run built.

Features

  • Midnight Run: a 100-car field on an endless night road, with rivals, cops, new road types and rain arriving as the run goes on.
  • Solo: eight districts, three of them mountain passes, with traffic density and race options for racers, cops, weather, repair pickups, a timed mode, and crashes off or Hardcore.
  • Online: matchmaking, plus private Crew rooms for 2 to 4 players.
  • Four cars with FWD, RWD and AWD handling, and automatic or manual shifting with overrev risk.
  • Loadout: three abilities (Nitrous, EMP, Siren), 14 aftermarket parts and 8 tire compounds.

Early Access

Velocity is playable now with Midnight Run, Solo, and early Crew and Online multiplayer. The plan for Early Access is more places to drive, cars, abilities and game modes, plus pink-slip races and local split-screen. These are plans, not promises, and player feedback will steer them.
