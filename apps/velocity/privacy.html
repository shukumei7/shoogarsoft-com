Velocity — Midnight Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

← Back to Velocity

Shoogar Soft Inc. ("we", "us") operates VELOCITY — Midnight Run ("the Game"), available on Steam, itch.io, CrazyGames, and Google Play. This page explains what information the Game collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

The Game does not require an account to play. Solo play, progress, and settings are stored on your device. They are not sent to us, apart from the anonymous usage data described below, which you can turn off.

Online features (multiplayer matchmaking, live sessions, and leaderboards) are powered by our own backend service ("velocity-net"). To provide these features, we collect:

  • Display name — a name you choose, shown to other players in matches and on leaderboards.
  • Run and match data — gameplay data such as score, completion time, car selected, difficulty, and map/challenge results, used to run matches and populate leaderboards.

This data is encrypted in transit. It is optional and under your control — it is only sent when you choose to use an online feature (multiplayer, leaderboards). It is used solely to provide app functionality (running matches, displaying leaderboards) and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Anonymous usage data (you can turn this off)

The demo versions of the Game on Steam and itch.io can send us anonymous usage data, so we can see where players quit, which settings players use, and which devices we need to support. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell or share it, and we do not use it to identify anyone.

You are in control. This is on by default. Open Settings and turn off SHARE ANONYMOUS USAGE DATA at any time; once it is off, the Game sends nothing further.

What is sent about your device and save (broad groups only, never exact values): your operating system family; whether you are on Steam or in a web browser; your input type (keyboard, gamepad or touch); your screen size group and display sharpness group; the graphics quality level the Game chose; your language (two letters); whether this is the demo or full build; and whether you have not yet finished a run on this save.

What is sent about your settings: gearbox, steering style, steering assist level, traffic density, whether racers, cops, no-crash, ghost, weather and the timer are on or off, graphics quality, and UI size.

What is sent as you play: which menu or screen you open; when a run starts and how it ends (finished, crashed, abandoned or game over); a short "still here" signal while the Game is open (every 15 seconds during a run, every 60 seconds on a menu); and when you close or hide the Game window.

What is not sent: no name, email, Steam ID, Steam profile name, leaderboard name or ID, save data, or typed text; no exact screen resolution, graphics card name, or device description. Our server does not store your IP address with this data. Like every connection to our server, it passes through Cloudflare's network.

Anonymous by design. Each time you start the Game it makes a new random code for that play session, and the code is never saved on your device. We cannot link one session to your next session, to your leaderboard entries, or to your save. For the same reason, we cannot find, correct or delete this data for you.

Where it goes and how long we keep it. It goes only to our own game server (the one that runs leaderboards and multiplayer matching), and no other company receives it. Raw events are deleted after 90 days.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share this data, including the anonymous usage data above, with third parties.

Data deletion

We do not currently offer a self-service deletion flow. To request deletion of data associated with your display name, contact us at support@shoogarsoft.com.

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's "Designed for Families" program. Content rating: ESRB Everyone 10+ (Fantasy Violence).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. Continued use of the Game after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
