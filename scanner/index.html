PlainsightScanner

What it is

PlainsightScanner is an automated accessibility checker run by Shoogar Soft Inc.

What it does

  • It loads a small number of public pages (usually the home page, a product, a collection or contact page, and the cart), plus robots.txt and the public /meta.json file (which states which country the store is in), with a 1.5-second pause between page scans.
  • We scan a store's public pages once. We don't re-scan a store unless its owner asks us to.
  • Never logs in, submits forms, or places orders.
  • Honors robots.txt, including rules written for PlainsightScanner.

User-agent

PlainsightScanner identifies itself with this user-agent string: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; PlainsightScanner/0.1; +https://shoogarsoft.com/scanner)

Opt out

To opt out, use either method.

Email: write to plainsight@shoogarsoft.com with "unsubscribe" or "opt out" and your store's domain. That adds the domain and your address to a permanent do-not-contact and do-not-scan list.

robots.txt: add these two lines to your robots.txt:

User-agent: PlainsightScanner
Disallow: /

Publishing

We don't publish any store's results without the owner's permission.
